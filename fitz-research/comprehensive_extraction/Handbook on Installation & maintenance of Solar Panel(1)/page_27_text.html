Step 12: Choose a Battery

Use Flooded Lead Acid Battery Cells

Nominal Voltage = 2 Volts No. of cells =24

Rated Capacity = 100 Amp-hrs

(3 Strings in Parallel)

Or

Nominal Voltage 6Volts, No. of Batteries = 8(series)

Rated Capacity 150 AH, 2 Strings in Parallel

Step 13: Choose Charge Controller

No. of Parallel Modules X Isc X 1.25 = Charge Controller Capacity

5 x 5x 1.25 = 31.25 Amps (Nearest higher available Value may be selected)

e.g. 48 Volts 40 Amps may be chosen.

Step 14: Choose Inverter

AC Watts on Inverter = Total AC Watts of AC devices X 25% extra.

Inverter of nearest higher rating may be chosen to cater for surge.

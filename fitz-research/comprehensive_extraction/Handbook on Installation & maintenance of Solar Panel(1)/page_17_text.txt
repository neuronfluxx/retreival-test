1.10.8   Operation 
Operation of the Solar power source is very simple. Once the system is installed, CHG. 
ON (Green) LED will glow during daytime and will indicate that the power is available 
for charging Battery Bank from SPV panel. Connect the equipment to be operated on solar 
power to the SPV Charge Control Unit at terminals marked ‘LOAD’ position. 
 
1.11      Types of Solar Panels  
Solar panels are classified on the basis of the following points : 
1) Crystalline Silicon (Mono/Poly/Amorphous) 
2) Different Size or Area of cells 
3) Type of cells & nos. (Rectangular/Circular/Square/ Pseudo-square/Semi-circular etc.) 
4) Power (High/Mid/Low range)

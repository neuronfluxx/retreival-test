1.9 Solar Panel

A Solar panel consists of a number of solar modules, which are connected in series and

parallel configuration to provide specific voltage and current to charge a battery. A diode is

connected on the +ve terminal of such string in forward bias. This is called Blocking diode.

This diode is provided so that in daytime current can flow from module to battery, but at

night or in cloudy day current should not flow back from battery to module or from one

string to another string Drawing shown in Fig 4 below illustrates a Solar panel.

3. Any obstruction (such as tree or building) should be avoided in East, West or South of

the place of installation. The following is the criteria:

(i) East or West: The distance between solar panel and obstruction should be more than

double the height of obstruction.

(ii) South: The distance should be more than half the height of obstruction.

4.

The support for the Solar panel need to be a robust one and should not be accessible

to general public. It should be so installed that rainwater, bird dropping, leaves etc. do

not accumulate and the top surface can be cleaned easily.

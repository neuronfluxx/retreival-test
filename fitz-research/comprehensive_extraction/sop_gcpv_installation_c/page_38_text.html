In TN-C earthing configuration, protective earth and neutral (PEN) conductors are combined in a single

conductor throughout the system. All exposed conductive parts are connected to the PEN conductor. For

three phase consumer, local earth electrode has to be provided in addition. Earthing of solar PV system

connected to this configuration can be connected to the main earthing terminal in switchboard.

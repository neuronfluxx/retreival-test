2.11 Step 11: Commissioning the system 
When you are sure that the conductors and connections are acceptable and the system has successfully 
passed the pre-commissioning testing, then it’s time to commission the system. 
 
Procedures to follow in sequence: 
(1) Refer to the inverter’s system manual and follow the start-up procedure 
(2) Check the inverter display and confirm that the solar array is supplying power to the utility grid. 
(3) Measure either the AC or DC current using a clamp meter and compare inverter output  
(4) Measure the DC input voltage and confirm that it is within the operating limits of the inverter 
(5) Measure the AC output voltage out of the inverter.

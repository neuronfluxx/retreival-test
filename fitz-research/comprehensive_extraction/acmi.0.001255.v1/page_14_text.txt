8.3.7 
Hands-on session 6 
322 
Hands-on session 6 extends on what participants have learnt over the course of the 
323 
workshop and introduces some different types of workflows. These include workflows 
324 
where more data cleaning is required, such as missing value handling and encoding of 
325 
categorical variables. Multiclass classification is also discussed. Additional workflows for 
326 
regression tasks, unsupervised learning, and computer vision are also introduced.

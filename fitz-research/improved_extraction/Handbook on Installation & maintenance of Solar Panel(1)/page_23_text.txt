 2.3.3 Load calculation 
   For DC Loads 
Load Amps  X  Operating Hours per Day  =    Amp Hour Per Day  (AHPD) 
(If Load is available in watts, divide the wattage by  system voltage to get  Amps) 
(Multiply by % duty cycle  if data available)   
 
  For AC Loads 
 AC Watts X Operating Hours per Day   = Watt Hours Per Day (WHPD) 
 AHPD = WHPD /  (Inverter Efficiency X  Nominal System Voltage)

2.3.5 Deciding Battery capacity 
  For calculating the capacity and number of  batteries, first the  number  of back up days 
are to be decided, based on No. of Consecutive sunless days. 
 
  For Example     Back up days for residential load =  3 to 5 days 
                            Back up days for Industrial Load  =   7 to 14 days 
                            Back up days for poor weather     =   7 to 14 days

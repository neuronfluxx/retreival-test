1.10.1  Solar Array 
Solar array consists of series/parallel combination of modules, which are mounted on the 
metallic structure in sunny and shadow free area at a fixed angle as recommended by 
designer. All the modules will face the South in Northern hemisphere. Cables from the 
array area will come to the control and battery room through junction boxes from panels of 
modules.
